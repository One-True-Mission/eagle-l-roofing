Accessibility Statement

Eagle-L Roofing & Remodeling LLC wants everyone to be able to use this website comfortably, including visitors who rely on assistive technology.

What We Do

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks.
  • Text alternatives on meaningful images.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, and controls.
  • Motion, such as the scrolling banner, respects your reduced-motion system setting.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort and we review the site as content changes. Our goal is to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA guidance where practical.

Feedback

If any part of this site is difficult for you to use, call us at (512) 888-3839 and we will help directly and fix the issue.
