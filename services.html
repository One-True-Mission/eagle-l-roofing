Six Ways We Can Help

Every service below starts the same way: a free inspection or consultation, a clear estimate, and a plan that works within your budget. Residential and commercial.

Roof Replacement

Eagle-L is qualified and certified by GAF to perform complete roofing replacements. When repair is no longer the smart money, we replace your roof with quality materials and certified installation practices.

  • Complete tear-off and replacement
  • GAF certified installation
  • Quality shingle and material options for every budget
  • Thorough cleanup, every day of the job

Roof Repair & Inspections

Leaks, wind damage, hail hits, and worn flashing rarely fix themselves. We find the real problem and repair it properly, starting with a free inspection and photos of what we find.

  • Free, no-pressure roof inspections
  • Leak diagnosis and repair
  • Storm and hail damage assessment
  • Shingle, flashing, and decking repair

Home Renovations

Kitchens, bathrooms, and whole-home updates designed around the way you actually live. Our in-house designers and tradespeople keep the vision and the build under one roof.

  • Kitchen and bathroom remodels
  • Whole-home updates and additions
  • Design help from an in-house team
  • Budgets respected from day one

Residential & Commercial Construction

More than a decade of residential and commercial projects across the Austin area. New construction, remodeling, and renovation for projects of any size, managed start to finish.

  • New construction and additions
  • Commercial remodels and build-outs
  • In-house project managers and engineers
  • One accountable team, start to finish

Masonry

Brick, stone, and concrete work that adds permanence and curb appeal. From repairs and repointing to new installations, our masons build to last.

  • Brick and stone work
  • Concrete flatwork and repair
  • Mailboxes, columns, and accents
  • Repair and restoration

Decks, Patios & Outdoor Living

  • Built for Central Texas weather
